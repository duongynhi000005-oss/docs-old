## Overview

[SparkFun MicroMod](https://www.sparkfun.com/micromod) is a modular hardware ecosystem that uses a standardized **67-pin M.2 (NGFF) E-Key connector** to pair interchangeable **Processor Boards** (ESP32, RP2040, STM32, nRF52840, SAMD51, Teensy) with specialized **Carrier Boards**.

Instead of redesigning your entire PCB when switching microcontrollers, MicroMod maps common microcontroller buses—Power, USB, UART, I2C, SPI, SDIO, CAN, I2S, ADC, PWM, and general-purpose I/O—to fixed positions on a 0.5 mm-pitch M.2 E-Key socket.

In this guide, you will learn:
1. How the MicroMod M.2 E-Key connector physical interface and pinout are organized.
2. How to define a reusable `MicroModSocket` component in **tscircuit** with full pin labels and schematic port grouping.
3. How to wire a complete **MicroMod Carrier Board** schematic (power decoupling, I2C pull-ups, reset circuit, and peripheral headers).

## 1. Understanding the MicroMod M.2 E-Key Interface

MicroMod uses a standard surface-mount **M.2 E-Key (Socket 1) connector** with a 0.5 mm pin pitch and a bevelled 22 mm × 22 mm processor card secured by an M2.5 × 3 mm machine screw into a 4.2 mm SMD standoff.

- **Odd pins (`1`–`75`)** sit on the **top row** of the M.2 connector and make contact with the top pads of the Processor Board.
- **Even pins (`2`–`74`)** sit on the **bottom row** of the M.2 connector and make contact with the bottom pads of the Processor Board.
- **E-Key Mechanical Notch**: Pins `24` through `31` are omitted to form the polarization key that prevents reversed or incompatible M.2 cards from being inserted.

### Core Pinout Summary (MicroMod Interface v1.0)

| Functional Group | M.2 Pin Numbers | MicroMod Signal Names | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Power & Ground** | `2`, `4` (`3.3V`), `70` (`VIN`), `74` (`RTC_3V`), `1`, `7`, `15`, `21`, `33`, `39`, `45`, `59`, `65`, `71`, `75` (`GND`) | `3.3V`, `VIN_5V`, `3.3V_EN`, `RTC_3V`, `GND` | Carrier supplies regulated `3.3V` (up to 1 A) and `3.3V_EN` control |
| **Boot, Reset & Status** | `5` (`STAT`), `44` (`BOOT`), `68` (`RESET#`) | `STAT`, `BOOT`, `RESET` | Active-low `RESET#` and `BOOT` with carrier pull-ups |
| **USB (Device / Host)** | `3` (`USB_D+`), `5` (`USB_D-`), `6` (`USB_VIN`) | `USB_DP`, `USB_DM`, `USB_VIN` | Differential 90 Ω pair to processor native USB |
| **Primary I2C + Interrupt** | `12` (`I2C_SDA`), `14` (`I2C_SCL`), `16` (`I2C_INT#`) | `I2C_SDA`, `I2C_SCL`, `I2C_INT` | Typically routed to Qwiic / STEMMA QT connector with 4.7 kΩ pull-ups |
| **Secondary I2C** | `50` (`I2C_SDA1`), `52` (`I2C_SCL1`) | `I2C_SDA1`, `I2C_SCL1` | Dedicated secondary I2C bus |
| **UART1 & UART2** | `17` (`TX1`), `19` (`RX1`), `20` (`RX2`), `22` (`TX2`), `18` (`CTS1`), `23` (`RTS1`) | `UART_TX1`, `UART_RX1`, `UART_RTS1`, `UART_CTS1`, `UART_TX2`, `UART_RX2` | Logic-level 3.3 V hardware serial ports |
| **Primary SPI** | `55` (`CS`), `57` (`SCK`), `59` (`MOSI`), `61` (`MISO`) | `SPI_CS`, `SPI_SCK`, `SPI_COPI`, `SPI_CIPO` | General peripheral SPI bus |
| **SDIO / Secondary SPI** | `60` (`SCK`), `62` (`CMD/COPI`), `64` (`DATA0/CIPO`), `66` (`DATA1`), `67` (`DATA2`), `69` (`DATA3/CS`) | `SDIO_SCK`, `SDIO_CMD`, `SDIO_DATA0`..`DATA3` | Used for microSD cards or secondary high-speed SPI flash |
| **CAN & Audio (I2S)** | `41` (`CAN_RX`), `43` (`CAN_TX`), `54` (`AUD_MCLK`), `56` (`AUD_OUT`), `58` (`AUD_IN`), `35` (`AUD_LRCLK`), `37` (`AUD_BCLK`) | `CAN_RX`, `CAN_TX`, `AUD_MCLK`, `AUD_OUT`, `AUD_IN`, `AUD_LRCLK`, `AUD_BCLK` |Requires external CAN transceiver on carrier board |
| **Analog, PWM & GPIO** | `34` (`A0`), `38` (`A1`), `32` (`PWM0`), `47` (`PWM1`), `10` (`D0`), `18` (`D1`), `40`–`73` (`G0`–`G10`) | `A0`, `A1`, `PWM0`, `PWM1`, `D0`, `D1`, `G0`..`G10` | 3.3 V max analog reference unless divided on carrier |

## 2. Creating a Reusable MicroMod M.2 Socket Component

We can model the 67-pin M.2 E-Key connector in **tscircuit** using `<chip />` with explicit `pinLabels` and `schPortArrangement` so that schematics remain clean and readable.

```tsx title="lib/MicroModConnector.tsx"

  pin75: "GND2",

  // USB & Status
  pin3: "USB_DP",
  pin5: "USB_DM",
  pin6: "LED_STAT",

  pin44: "BOOT_N",

  pin12: "I2C_SDA",
  pin14: "I2C_SCL",
  pin16: "I2C_INT_N",
  pin50: "I2C_SDA1",
  pin52: "I2C_SCL1",

  pin19: "UART_RX1",
```

## 3. Building a MicroMod Carrier Board Circuit

When designing a MicroMod Carrier Board, there are four essential support subcircuits to include around the M.2 socket:

1. **Local 3.3V Bypassing**: Place a `10uF` bulk capacitor and a `0.1uF` ceramic decoupling capacitor close to pins `2` and `4` (`V3_3`) of the M.2 connector.
2. **I2C Pull-Up Network**: Add `4.7kΩ` pull-up resistors from `I2C_SDA` (pin `12`) and `I2C_SCL` (pin `14`) to `3.3V` so connected Qwiic / I2C sensors work across all processor modules.
3. **Reset & Boot Pull-Ups**: Pull `RESET_N` (pin `68`) and `BOOT_N` (pin `44`) up to `3.3V` via `10kΩ` resistors so tactile buttons can pull them to `GND`.
4. **Standoff Mounting Hole**: Place a grounded `<platedhole />` 22 mm away from the M.2 socket centerline to accept the M2.5 standoff that locks the processor module in place.

```tsx title="MicroModCarrierBoard.tsx"
import { MicroModConnector } from "./lib/MicroModConnector"

export default () => (
  <board width="50mm" height="45mm">
    {/* 67-pin M.2 E-Key Socket */}
    <MicroModConnector name="J1" schX={0} schY={0} pcbX={0} pcbY={-10} />

    {/* M2.5 Standoff Mounting Hole for Processor Module (22mm from socket) */}
    <platedhole
      name="H1"
      shape="circle"
      holeDiameter="2.7mm"
      outerDiameter="5.0mm"
      pcbX={0}
      pcbY={12}
    />

    {/* 3.3V Decoupling Capacitors */}
    <capacitor
      name="C1"
      capacitance="10uF"
      footprint="0805"
      schX={-5}
      schY={2}
      pcbX={-12}
      pcbY={-10}
    />
    <capacitor
      name="C2"
      capacitance="0.1uF"
      footprint="0402"
      schX={-3.5}
      schY={2}
      pcbX={-9}
      pcbY={-10}
    />

    {/* Primary I2C Bus Pull-Up Resistors */}
    <resistor
      name="R_SDA"
      resistance="4.7k"
      footprint="0402"
      schX={4}
      schY={3}
      pcbX={10}
      pcbY={-8}
    />
    <resistor
      name="R_SCL"
      resistance="4.7k"
      footprint="0402"
      schX={5.5}
      schY={3}
      pcbX={10}
      pcbY={-5}
    />

    {/* Active-Low Reset Pull-Up & Status LED */}
    <resistor
      name="R_RST"
      resistance="10k"
      footprint="0402"
      schX={-4}
      schY={-1}
      pcbX={-10}
      pcbY={-5}
    />
    <resistor
      name="R_STAT"
      resistance="1k"
      footprint="0402"
      schX={4}
      schY={-3}
      pcbX={10}
      pcbY={5}
    />
    <led
      name="D_STAT"
      footprint="0603"
      schX={6}
      schY={-3}
      pcbX={14}
      pcbY={5}
    />

    {/* Qwiic / I2C Expansion Header */}
    <pinheader
      name="J_I2C"
      pinCount={4}
      footprint="pinrow4"
      schX={7.5}
      schY={2.5}
      pcbX={18}
      pcbY={-6}
    />

    {/* Power & Decoupling Traces */}
    <trace from=".J1 > .V3_3" to="net.V3_3" />
    <trace from=".J1 > .V3_3_2" to="net.V3_3" />
    <trace from=".C1 > .pos" to="net.V3_3" />
    <trace from=".C1 > .neg" to="net.GND" />
    <trace from=".C2 > .pos" to="net.V3_3" />
    <trace from=".C2 > .neg" to="net.GND" />
    <trace from=".J1 > .GND1" to="net.GND" />
    <trace from=".J1 > .GND2" to="net.GND" />

    {/* Reset Pull-Up */}
    <trace from=".R_RST > .pin1" to="net.V3_3" />
    <trace from=".R_RST > .pin2" to=".J1 > .RESET_N" />

    {/* I2C Pull-Ups and Qwiic Header Connections */}
    <trace from=".R_SDA > .pin1" to="net.V3_3" />
    <trace from=".R_SDA > .pin2" to=".J1 > .I2C_SDA" />
    <trace from=".R_SCL > .pin1" to="net.V3_3" />
    <trace from=".R_SCL > .pin2" to=".J1 > .I2C_SCL" />
    <trace from=".J_I2C > .pin1" to="net.GND" />
    <trace from=".J_I2C > .pin2" to="net.V3_3" />
    <trace from=".J_I2C > .pin3" to=".J1 > .I2C_SDA" />
    <trace from=".J_I2C > .pin4" to=".J1 > .I2C_SCL" />

    {/* Status LED Connection */}
    <trace from=".J1 > .LED_STAT" to=".R_STAT > .pin1" />
    <trace from=".R_STAT > .pin2" to=".D_STAT > .pos" />
    <trace from=".D_STAT > .neg" to="net.GND" />
  </board>
)
```

## 4. Layout & Mechanical Design Checklist

When laying out either a MicroMod Carrier Board or a Processor Module PCB:

- **Connector Part Numbers**:
  - **Standard 4.2 mm Height M.2 E-Key Socket**: Amphenol `MDT420E01001` or TE Connectivity `2199230-4`.
  - **SMD Standoff (4.2 mm Height, M2.5 Thread)**: Wurth Elektronik `9774025243R` placed `22.0 mm` from the connector datum line.
- **High-Speed Differential Routing**:
  - Route `USB_DP` (pin `3`) and `USB_DM` (pin `5`) as a length-matched 90 Ω differential pair with minimal via transitions.
- **Analog Voltage Limits**:
  - MicroMod analog inputs (`A0`, `A1`) are strictly **3.3 V maximum** across all processor families. Always place a resistive voltage divider or op-amp buffer on the Carrier Board if measuring 5 V or battery voltages.
